§ About Founded 2024 · DC · MD
A US-incorporated, US-controlled energy company

Take the chemistry that works in a beaker.

Caldera Voltaics commercializes a National Laboratory breakthrough in lithium-sulfur chemistry. We are headquartered in Washington, DC, with technical operations in Laurel, Maryland and an exclusive commercial license to 17 patented proprietary technologies.

01 · Mandate

Ship it in volume from a US-allied supply chain.

Caldera Voltaics has a single mandate: take a chemistry that works in a beaker, and ship it in volume from a US-allied supply chain. We are building the cell platform that lets the West stop importing the variable that decides every mission.

Cell-level performance has been validated at a National Laboratory under repeatable test protocol. The next several years are about converting that validation into ITAR-aware production, in steps that match the procurement reality of the customers who need the cell first.
