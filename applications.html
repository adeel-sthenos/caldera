§ Applications Three classes · sequenced
Where energy density is the binding constraint

Where Wh/kg
decides the mission.

Caldera targets three application classes in deliberate sequence: defense, aerospace, and long-haul mobility. Each is a market where energy density at the cell decides whether the platform is feasible at all.

01 · Defense

Underwater.
Airborne. Distributed.

Underwater and aerial autonomous platforms where a 50% mass reduction at iso-energy translates directly into range, loiter time, and payload capacity the incumbent chemistry cannot deliver. Caldera operates with ITAR-aware processes from the first kilogram.

02 · Aerospace

Lighter cells.
Longer loiter.

For platforms that pay every additional kilogram in lift, a lighter cell is not a feature — it is the design constraint. Caldera unlocks loiter durations and payload masses that close previously infeasible mission profiles in HALE, communications relay, and persistent observation.

03 · Mobility & grid

Tonne-kilometers,
per kilogram of pack.

Class 8 trucking and logistics fleets where the binding metric is tonne-kilometers per kilogram of pack. A lighter, US-sourced cell, with a sulfur feedstock that does not depend on cobalt or nickel, changes the unit economics of long-haul electrification.

Application sequencing

Defense first.
Aerospace next. Mobility at scale.

Sequencing is set by where energy density is most binding and where unit pricing supports an early-volume cell platform. Mobility is the long-arc market: the same cell, at scale, on different lines.
