01 · The chemistry

An additive that unlocks sulfur.

Sulfur is the cathode the industry has wanted for thirty years. It is light, abundant, and a refining byproduct sourced overwhelmingly inside US-allied geographies.

Caldera Voltaics has developed a proprietary additive that unlocks the electrochemical potential of sulfur. The manufacturing process changes by exactly one input: a swap at the cathode-coating step.
