How we work

Hands-on. On-site. Real deadlines.

01 · Where

DC & Laurel, MD

On-site by default. Cell test, R&D, and National Laboratory collaboration in Laurel; commercial, government affairs, and finance in DC. Same corridor, 32 miles apart.

02 · Pace

Programs, not projects

Customer pilots and procurement timelines set the cadence. We commit to dates and we hit them. Decisions are made by the people closest to the cell.

03 · Compensation

Cash, equity, ITAR

Market cash, meaningful early-stage equity, full benefits. ITAR-eligible roles require US person status; we'll tell you up front which ones do.
